## 🚀 Active Risk Prototypes

### 1. [fraud-rule-autopsy](#)
**The Framework:** A controlled environment for stress-testing proposed fraud rules against simulated customer scenarios to surface false positives, missed fraud, and edge cases.
**Approach:** Generates fraudulent, legitimate, and ambiguous customer behaviors around proposed rules, then evaluates where a rule correctly identifies risk, incorrectly flags legitimate behavior, or fails to detect a relevant pattern.
`Rule Stress Testing` `False Positive Analysis` `Simulated Scenarios`

### 2. [fraud-signal-contradiction](#)
**The Framework:** A behavioral analysis prototype evaluating whether fraud signals remain meaningful once historical customer context is introduced.
**Approach:** Generates scenarios where multiple signals initially appear suspicious, then introduces behavioral context that strengthens, weakens, or contradicts the initial risk assessment — demonstrating how additive scoring can exaggerate risk when signals reflect the same underlying behavior.
`Behavioral Context` `Signal Dependency` `False Positive Reduction`

### 3. [what-would-change-my-mind](#)
**The Framework:** An investigative reasoning prototype that stress-tests a working fraud hypothesis by surfacing supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, missing information, and alternative explanations.
**Approach:** Runs investigations across ATO, APP, Synthetic Identity, Money Mule, and Bustout scenarios, identifying the next investigative question or piece of evidence most likely to change the current conclusion.
`Hypothesis Testing` `Evidence Analysis` `Investigative Reasoning`
